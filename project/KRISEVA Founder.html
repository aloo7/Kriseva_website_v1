Founder Note

Where the problem was first observed.

Before IIT Gandhinagar, time spent in defense marketing and operations, electronic warfare, anti-drone, radar adjacent products, surfaced a pattern that did not look like a tooling gap on the surface, but underneath it was.

OBSERVED Procurement bottlenecks were not a side-issue, they were a daily, structural problem. Manual discovery across portals, missed tenders, and documentation cycles that consumed disproportionate operator time.
OBSERVED Tenders were missed not because tools did not exist, but because the work between an alert and a defensible BID / REVIEW / SKIP decision was still being done by a person, in a hurry, against a deadline.
OBSERVED Technical review was scattered: an analyst would forward a bundle, a reviewer would read it on their own time, compliance would be reconstructed from memory and a tab-switching pass through the document.
INFERRED The shared primitive across these workflows is structured tender intelligence, source-linked, evidence-backed, operator-reviewed, not another notification feed.

Source: founder origin notes · Prior employer details kept to safe published wording

Why KRISEVA

The problem is not that companies lack another alert tool.
The problem is that tender decisions are hidden inside document bundles.

Across the workflows surveyed, listings and notifications are not the bottleneck, extraction, eligibility cross-check, compliance reconstruction, and bid-readiness review are. KRISEVA is being built as procurement intelligence for that work, specifically.

TAS: the Tender Analysis System, begins on the bidder side because that pain is immediate, validated, and operator-shaped. Issuer-side workflows are on the roadmap, not in the product.

NOT A generic AI assistant for tenders.
NOT A notification feed that summarizes listings.
NOT An autonomous bid-submission system.
IS Procurement intelligence, defense-procurement specific, evidence-linked, operator-reviewed.

Operating Principles 05 principles · enforced in product & copy

How the work gets made, and what it refuses to be.

These are not aspirational values. They are operating constraints, applied to the product surface, the marketing surface, and stakeholder conversations alike.

01

Evidence over hype

Every claim in a brief must point back to a clause and a page. Marketing claims on this site are status-labeled. We publish what is observed, what is validated, and what is roadmap, separately.

Claim discipline · Source trails

02

Local-first where appropriate

Tender bundles and operator data stay on local storage and local model routes where configured. Cloud routes are explicit, not default. The boundary is documented, not assumed.

Local storage · Local model routes

03

Human-reviewed recommendations

KRISEVA recommends; the operator decides. BID / REVIEW / SKIP is a defensible recommendation with a source trail, not an autonomous action against a portal.

Operator authority · No autonomous bid

04

Claim discipline

No government-approved, DRDO-approved, CRPF-approved, Indian-Army-validated language. No fake metrics, no fake logos, no testimonial carousel. Anonymized stakeholders stay anonymized.

No endorsement claimed or implied

05

Defense procurement specificity

The product is built for defense procurement workflows, bundle anatomy, eligibility logic, compliance tables, not for generic tender automation. Specificity is the moat, not the limitation.

Bidder-side first · Issuer-side roadmap
