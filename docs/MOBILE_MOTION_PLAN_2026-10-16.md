# Mobile film and bridge experience

Date: 16 October 2026. This plan incorporates the founder's later request for a 15 to 30 second illustrative film.

## Problem and chosen direction

The old landscape panorama rendered SVG labels at roughly 3 CSS pixels on an ordinary phone. The supplied Android screenshot also appears consistent with a wider browser layout scaled onto a phone. The screenshot alone does not identify the actual browser setting.

The main explanation is now a 24-second, silent-first motion film. It is composed separately at 720 by 900 for phones and 1280 by 720 for wider displays. Strong sans typography, near-white page surfaces and navy film scenes replace the small editorial panorama as the first impression. The detailed SVG remains available at `/bridge` with focused camera views.

## Implementation

1. Research the three exact X references and primary repositories. Install useful, compatible tools with recorded versions and licenses.
2. Author an original, deterministic GSAP timeline and render both formats with Hyperframes 0.8.163. Keep local source assets and an editable composition.
3. Embed local H.264 MP4s with an explicit play action, native controls, captions, chapter buttons, a transcript and direct product paths. Preserve playback position across format changes. No automatic playback or audio.
4. Move the detailed bridge to `/bridge`. Use Motion for camera interpolation and a bounded, optional Paper shader for atmosphere. Pause hidden and offscreen work. Keep complete HTML/SVG when JavaScript, WebGL or motion is unavailable.
5. Adapt unusually wide layouts on small touch screens without changing browser zoom. Keep normal portrait, landscape and enlarged text readable.
6. Run production-build checks, Chromium/WebKit/Firefox regression, 13-route reflow checks, film runtime/layout/contrast checks and visual review of local evidence. Keep physical-phone and real browser desktop-mode checks explicit as outstanding human review.

## Delivery boundaries

A 24-second runtime is verified. Understanding, engagement and conversion have not been measured with visitors. This revision is delivered as a reviewable branch and PR. The existing production homepage remains on the previously merged release until the founder requests this revision to be merged.
